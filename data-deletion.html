User Data Deletion

Flicklax Corporation — AI Assistant on Facebook Messenger

Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can request deletion of the personal data we hold about you from the Flicklax AI Assistant. This page explains how, and what happens next.

How to request deletion

Option A — Email (preferred). Email flicklaxcorporation@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Include the Facebook name you used to message our Page and, if possible, a screenshot of the conversation so we can locate your record. We never ask for your password.

Option B — In Messenger. Send the word DELETE MY DATA to our Facebook Page. We will confirm and process your request.

What we delete

  • Your page-scoped user ID (PSID) mapping and profile fields we stored;
  • Conversation content and stored message IDs;
  • Property preferences and any viewing/booking records not required for legal or accounting purposes.

We may retain the minimum needed to comply with law (e.g. financial records) and to prevent abuse; such data is minimised and access-restricted.

Timeline

We acknowledge requests within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation when done.

Meta's automatic deletion callback

If you remove the app / revoke access on Facebook, Meta may notify us through its Data Deletion Callback. We process such notifications and delete the associated data on the same timeline.
