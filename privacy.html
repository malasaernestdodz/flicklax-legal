3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and operate the AI assistant;
  • To match your stated preferences against property listings and suggest viewings;
  • To create, reschedule or cancel viewings and notify our agents;
  • To prevent spam/abuse, rate-limit and secure the Service;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Legal bases

Where required (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR): performance of a contract or steps at your request (answering you, arranging viewings); legitimate interests (operating and securing the Service); consent (where you opt in); and legal obligation.

5. Sharing & processors

We share data only as needed to run the Service, with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — Facebook Messenger delivery (message transport).
  • Hosting / automation — our self-hosted n8n workflow engine and databases.
  • Cloud infrastructure — servers and databases used to run the Service.
  • AI model providers — to generate replies, message content is sent to an OpenAI-compatible language-model endpoint. No data is used to train third-party models.
  • Our real-estate agents — limited to what is needed for a viewing you requested.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside your country. Where required we rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

7. Retention

  • Conversation & preference data: kept while we serve you and for up to 24 months afterwards, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Operational/security logs: up to 90 days.
  • Booking records: kept as required for contractual/legal purposes.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these, see User Data Deletion or email flicklaxcorporation@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), scoped API credentials and least-privilege principles. No system is perfectly secure; we notify affected users and authorities where required.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy; the "Last updated" date will change and material changes will be announced on this page or in chat.
